Privacy Policy

Digittouch Shorts, by Digittouch Ent. Effective 26 October 2026.

Who we are

Digittouch Shorts is a private publishing tool operated by Digittouch Ent to manage its own YouTube channel, Mind Box. Contact: digittouch.ceo@gmail.com.

Google user data we access

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the tool accesses the Mind Box YouTube account through the YouTube Data API to upload videos and to set or update their titles, descriptions, tags, visibility and publishing schedule.

How we use it

We use this access only to publish and manage videos on the Mind Box channel, as requested by the channel owner. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

How we store and protect it

Access tokens are stored securely and used only by the tool. We do not store the content of the Google account beyond what is needed to publish videos. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone.

Limited Use

Digittouch Shorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
