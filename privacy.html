Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Mind Box Publisher is used only by the owner of the Mind Box TikTok account to upload and publish his own videos.

What we access: the account's basic profile (name, avatar, ID) and permission to upload and publish videos to that same account.

How it is used: only to post the owner's own Mind Box videos with their captions. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

Storage: the sign-in token is kept securely by the integration service Composio so posts can be scheduled. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

Removal: access can be removed at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
